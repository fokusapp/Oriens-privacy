Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Your data stays on your device

Oriens does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All your habits, journal entries, and bucket list items are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

No accounts required

Oriens works completely offline. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect any identifying information.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers.

Third-party services

Oriens does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
