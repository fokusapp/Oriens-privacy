Privacy Policy for Oriens

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Overview

Oriens is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. All information you enter into Oriens (habits, journal entries, bucket list items, countries visited, etc.) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

Data Storage

Your data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to external servers. We have no access to your data.

iCloud (if applicable)

If you have iCloud enabled, your data may sync across your Apple devices through Apple's iCloud service. This is handled entirely by Apple, and we do not have access to this data. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for more information.

Notifications

Oriens may send you local notifications if you enable reminders for your habits. These notifications are generated locally on your device and do not involve any external services.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect usage data.

Third-Party Services

Oriens does not use any third-party services that collect data.

Children's Privacy

Oriens does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app is suitable for all ages and stores all data locally.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
